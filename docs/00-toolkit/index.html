Units and Dimensional Analysis

Length scales linearly, surface area scales with the square of length, and volume (so mass, for a fixed density) scales with the cube of length. Drag the slider and watch why a scaled-up elephant can't just be a scaled-up mouse — its legs would need to grow far thicker than its body just to keep up with its own weight.

Scale factor k1.0×
Length ratiok = 1.00
Area ratiok² = 1.00
Volume / mass ratiok³ = 1.00

At k = 1.0, both objects are identical: everything scales together.

Reference Frames

A walker moves along a moving walkway. The same motion looks different depending on whose frame you describe it from: the ground frame sees both the walkway and the walker's own steps; the walkway frame only sees the steps.

Walkway speed, u1.0 m/s
Walking speed (relative to walkway), w1.0 m/s
Ground-frame velocityu + w = 2.0 m/s
Walkway-frame velocityw = 1.0 m/s
